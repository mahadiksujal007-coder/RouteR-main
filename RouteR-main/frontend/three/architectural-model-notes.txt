RouteR procedural tower visualization

Design status
- Original four-arm radial high-rise visualization with stepped setbacks.
- Not a reconstruction of Burj Khalifa or any other named building.
- Architectural visualization only; not BIM, a survey model, or construction documentation.
- No structural safety, code compliance, or engineering approval is implied.

Implemented visualization dimensions
- Ground datum to spire tip: 400 m.
- Podium: 20 m.
- Main tower: 300 m.
- Segmented spire: 80 m.
- Landscape site: 240 m square.
- Visual floor bands: 120.
- Radial arms: 4; facade bays per arm: 3.
- Central core: 8-sided; setback interval: 6 visual floor bands.
- Setback depth: 1.5 m per staggered visual retraction.
- Full facade mode: 2,880 generated glazing panels.

These are authored visualization parameters. The podium, floor-band, setback,
core, wing, and site dimensions are not claims about a real building and have
not been derived from a dimensioned architectural drawing. The geometry is
deliberately distinct from the user-supplied reference: it uses four radial
arms, an eight-sided core, three facade bays per arm, and different massing
proportions.

Source and fact handling
- The user-provided brief names 828 m as the reference building height and
  supplies source links below. The project does not independently verify those
  source statements or use them to reproduce a building.
- SOM project page: https://www.som.com/projects/burj-khalifa/
- Architecture and design: https://www.burjkhalifa.ae/the-tower/architecture-design/
- Structural information: https://www.burjkhalifa.ae/the-tower/structures/
- Official fact sheet: https://www.burjkhalifa.ae/img/fact-sheet.pdf
- CTBUH technical paper: https://global.ctbuh.org/paper/310

The generated geometry is built locally from reusable Three.js geometry and
materials. Station coordinates remain [longitude, latitude] and are placed
using Mapbox Mercator coordinates. The same 400 m master geometry is instanced
at eligible stations currently in the map viewport. Simplified geometry is
used at intermediate 3D zoom; facade detail is enabled at close zoom.
